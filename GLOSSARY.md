# Glossary

**Vacancy**
A job opening that a Recruiter publishes on the platform.

**Recruiter**
A user with an account who creates and manages Vacancies. Only the Recruiter who created a Vacancy can edit or delete it.

**Job Seeker**
A visitor without an account who searches Vacancies by title and views their details.

**Company**
The employer identity (name, business sector, size) shown on a Vacancy. It belongs to the Recruiter, not to the Vacancy.

**Position**
The role a Vacancy hires for, chosen from a fixed list. Distinct from the Vacancy's title, which is free text.

**Active Until**
The last day a Vacancy accepts candidates.
